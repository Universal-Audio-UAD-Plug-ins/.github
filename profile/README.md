## 👀 At a Glance
Universal Audio UAD for Windows is a set of plugins that run inside your DAW as VST, AU, or AAX inserts rather than as a standalone program. Once installed, every title appears in your project's effects list, ready to add analog-modeled tone to any channel. A number of free UAD plugins are available too, so you can start hearing the sound before committing to larger bundles.

## 🎁 What You Get
| Feature | What it does for you |

| Compressors and limiters | Control dynamics and add punch with faithful hardware recreations. |
| Equalizers | Shape tone using EQ curves modeled on classic studio units. |

## ⚡ Quick Start
1. Open your DAW and load a session you want to mix.
2. Drop a Universal Audio UAD plugin onto a track or bus.
3. Pick a preset close to the sound you want.
4. Fine-tune the controls during playback, then save the chain with your project.

## ❓ FAQ
**Are there free Universal Audio UAD plugins?**
Yes. While the full Universal Audio UAD catalog is a paid ecosystem, Universal Audio offers a set of free UAD plugins so you can get started, and paid titles can often be trialed before purchase.

**Which versions of Windows are supported?**
The plugins run on modern 64-bit Windows, including Windows 10 and Windows 11, inside a supported DAW.

**Is my data secure?**
Your projects and audio stay in your DAW on your computer. Activation is handled through your Universal Audio account, so keep your credentials protected.
